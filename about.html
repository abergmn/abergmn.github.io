About Me

I’m Aaron Bergmann, a second-year Information Technology major with a Cyber Security minor at Salem State University. Originally from Gloucester, Massachusetts, I’ve been immersed in tech my entire life — constantly building, breaking, and fixing systems just to understand how they work. My interests lie in the hands-on, technical side of IT, with a focus on system administration, backend development, and network infrastructure.


At Salem State, I work as a Network Technician within the university's networking department, gaining hands-on experience with enterprise networking, VLANs, DHCP configurations, and more. I also actively host small-scale services and test infrastructure setups — even within the limitations of university housing.

What I Do

I maintain a personal homelab consisting of a Dell PowerEdge R710, R300, and OptiPlex 3040 Micro — all running in a Proxmox cluster. I use this setup to experiment with virtualization, host services like Pi-hole, TrueNAS, Jellyfin, and run OPNsense for firewall testing. I also develop tools and software in Node.js and Python, including CLI tools, automation scripts, and Electron-based desktop apps.

Skills & Tools

  • Python scripting & automation
  • Node.js backend development

  • Cloudflare (DNS, Tunnels, Workers)

Projects & Experience

I use my homelab to test and deploy services in virtual machines, simulate small network environments, and troubleshoot real-world scenarios. I’ve hosted DHCP servers, reverse proxies, media servers, web servers, and mail servers — often setting everything up from scratch. On the dev side, I build tools in Node.js and Python that help automate workflows or provide utility.


Check out my code on GitHub. Many of my contributions happen under alt accounts, but this profile contains a mix of public projects and experiments.

Personal Interests

Outside of tech, I spend time outdoors — fishing and hiking when I can. I enjoy taking time away from the screen to reset, think, and explore. I’m methodical and objective by nature — I enjoy dissecting problems, designing systems, and solving issues with precision and intention.


I’m not big on surface-level details or generic labels — I care about doing things the right way, learning continuously, and pushing myself to build things that are functional, secure, and maintainable.

Down the road, I’m interested in returning for a business-related degree and eventually starting my own company focused on network infrastructure, offering services like small business network design, IP CCTV setups, and custom IT deployments.


I’m currently looking for internships or part-time roles in networking, systems administration, or IT infrastructure. If you're interested in working together, feel free to reach out.
